Frequently asked questions

Does Dragon Champions require an account?

The current version does not require an online account. App information is stored locally on the device.

How do I add or change a chore?

Open the relevant profile or chore area, then use the add or edit controls provided in the App.

How are points and rewards handled?

Points are used as a motivational tool inside the App. Parents or guardians decide which rewards are available and when they are earned.

Why am I not receiving reminders?

Confirm that notifications are enabled for Dragon Champions in the device settings and that the reminder is scheduled correctly.

Where is my information stored?

The current version stores profile, chore, reward, and preference information locally on the device. Uninstalling the App or clearing its storage may remove that information.

How do I delete local app information?

Delete the relevant profile or entries in the App when available. You can also clear the App's storage through the device settings or uninstall it.
