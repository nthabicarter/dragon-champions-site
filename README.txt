DRAGON CHAMPIONS WEBSITE

1. Replace every occurrence of:
   YOUR-SUPPORT-EMAIL@example.com
   with your real support email.

2. Open index.html in a browser to preview the site.

3. Upload the entire folder contents to a GitHub repository.

4. In GitHub: Settings > Pages > Deploy from a branch > main > /(root) > Save.

5. Your privacy-policy URL will look like:
   https://YOUR-GITHUB-USERNAME.github.io/REPOSITORY-NAME/privacy-policy.html

6. Also add the same privacy-policy link inside the Android app before submitting, because apps targeting children must provide the link in the store listing and in the app.

IMPORTANT:
The Privacy Policy describes the current offline/local-storage version. Update it and the Google Play Data safety form before releasing online accounts, cloud sync, analytics, advertising, or other data-collecting features.
