Privacy Policy

How Dragon Champions handles information in the current app version.

Last updated: July 25, 2026

Before publishing: replace YOUR-SUPPORT-EMAIL@example.com with an email address you actively monitor.

1. Overview

Dragon Champions ("the App," "we," "us," or "our") is a family-friendly chore and rewards application. This Privacy Policy explains what information the current version of the App uses, how that information is handled, and the choices available to users and parents or guardians.

2. Information handled by the current version

The current version does not require an online account and does not send names, email addresses, phone numbers, chore information, reward information, or profile information to a server operated by us.

The App may store the following information locally on the user's device:

  • Profile names or nicknames entered by the user
  • Chores, completion status, points, and rewards
  • App preferences and settings
  • Notification preferences
  • A selected profile image, when the user chooses to add one

This locally stored information is used only to provide the App's features on that device. Removing the App or clearing its storage may delete this information.

3. Photos and media

If a user chooses to add a profile image, the App may request permission to select an image from the device. Access is used only after the user chooses this feature. The current version does not upload selected images to our servers.

4. Notifications

The App may request permission to send local notifications for chore reminders or related app activities. These reminders are scheduled and displayed by the user's device. Users can change notification permissions in their device settings.

5. Text-to-speech and external links

The App may use device text-to-speech capabilities to read certain content aloud. It may also open external webpages through the device browser when a user selects a link. Any external site is governed by its own privacy policy.

6. Children's privacy

Dragon Champions is intended for family use and may be used by children with parent or guardian involvement. The current version is designed to avoid collecting personal information through an online service operated by us. Parents and guardians should supervise information entered into the App and may remove local information by editing the relevant profile, clearing the App's storage, or uninstalling the App.

We are committed to complying with applicable children's privacy requirements and the Google Play Families policies for versions distributed through Google Play.

7. Data sharing and sale

We do not sell personal information. In the current version, we do not share locally stored chore, reward, profile, or preference information with third parties.

8. Data security

Information stored locally is protected by the safeguards provided by the user's device and operating system. No method of electronic storage is completely secure, so users should protect their devices with appropriate security controls.

9. Future account features

A future version may introduce online accounts or cloud synchronization. Before such features are released, this Privacy Policy and the App's Google Play Data safety disclosures will be updated to describe the information collected, its purpose, retention, deletion options, and any service providers involved.

10. User choices and deletion

Users can manage App permissions in their device settings. Local App information can generally be removed by deleting profiles or entries within the App, clearing the App's storage, or uninstalling the App.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App changes or when legal and platform requirements change. The updated policy will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.
